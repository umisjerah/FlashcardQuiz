Flashcard Quiz

Flashcard Quiz is a simple and user-friendly study application designed to help students review lessons, memorize important information, and test their knowledge. It allows users to create flashcard decks, study questions and answers, and take quizzes to improve their learning. It also tracks learning progress so students can monitor their performance.

Features

- Dashboard – Displays the total decks, flashcards, mastered cards, and quiz sessions.
- My Decks – Create, organize, search, and manage flashcard decks by subject.
- Study Mode – Review flashcards and reveal answers to test your memory.
- Quiz Mode – Test your knowledge using Multiple Choice or Written Recall.
- Quiz Settings – Choose the number of questions and question order.
- My Progress – Track reviewed cards, mastered cards, quiz accuracy, and learning status.
- Settings – Customize your student profile and preferred study label.
- Backup and Restore – Keep a backup of your decks, flashcards, and study progress.
- Local Storage – Automatically saves your study data on your device.

How to Install

1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge).
2. Visit the application website: https://flashcardqz.netlify.app
3. Wait for the application to load.
4. Go to the Settings section.
5. Find the Install this app option.
6. Click the Install button.
7. Once installed, you can access the application from your desktop or home screen.

How to Run

1. Open the Flashcard Quiz application.
2. Go to the Dashboard to see your study overview.
3. Click Create New Deck or go to My Decks to create a flashcard deck.
4. Enter your subject and add your questions and answers.
5. Go to Study to review your flashcards.
6. Click or reveal each card to see its answer.
7. Go to Quiz to test your knowledge.
8. Choose your preferred quiz mode and number of questions.
9. Click Start Quiz to begin answering.
10. Visit My Progress to check your learning performance.
